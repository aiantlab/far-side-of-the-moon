NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.
Visualizer: Ernie Wright.

Source products used:
- lroc_color_2k.jpg, derived from LROC Wide Angle Camera color data.
- ldem_3_8bit.jpg, derived from LRO Laser Altimeter elevation data.

The project converts these products to smaller WebP files for browser
rendering. The original downloads are retained under work/assets/nasa/.
